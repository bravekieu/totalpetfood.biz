Fishmeal

Originated from highly digestible protein, amino acids, unsaturated fatty acids and minerals ideal for complete diets. A source of biological valued protein, fishmeal has a balanced profile of essential amino acids. Fishmeal also serves as a supply of essential omega-3 fatty acids, which make many fish so valuable. Its composition is basically seafish by-product. It is effective ingredient of feed for aquatic, swine, bovine, poultry and pet food also.
