Hemoglobin

Hemoglobin protein from pork/beef blood obtained by fractionation and spraydrying. This protein component is very high and is characterised by its excellent digestibility and its high organic iron It is effective ingredient of feed for aquatic, swine, bovine, poultry and pet food.
